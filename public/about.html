About VANYA

Our Story

VANYA was born from a simple belief: every girl deserves fashion that looks fabulous, feels comfortable, and costs a fair price. We started in a small studio with a handful of designs and a big dream — to become the go-to wardrobe partner for the new generation.

Today we craft trendy dresses, tops, ethnic wear, bottoms and winter essentials that blend contemporary style with Indian sensibilities. Every piece is designed in-house, quality-checked by hand, and priced honestly — no inflated MRPs, no hidden charges.
